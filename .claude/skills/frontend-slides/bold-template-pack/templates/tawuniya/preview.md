## Selection Metadata

- Slug: `tawuniya`
- Tagline: Vivid violet on white, deep navy-violet weight, and rationed mint confidence — a brand-accurate insurance/financial system built from Tawuniya's own theme.
- Mood: trustworthy, modern, confident, institutional-but-warm
- Tone: clear, considered, brand-precise, unmistakably corporate-yet-contemporary
- Formality: medium-high
- Density: medium
- Scheme: light (with dedicated deep-navy full-bleed surfaces for dividers/close)
- Best for: Anything that should read as insurance-, finance-, or institution-grade while staying visually modern: client-facing proposals, quarterly/board updates, product or policy explainers, partner and broker decks, internal strategy reviews. Purpose-built for Tawuniya-branded material, but equally usable by other insurance, financial-services, or B2B institutional brands that want a violet-led (rather than navy-led) trust palette.
- Avoid for: Contexts that want to feel playful, handmade, or maximalist — the single-accent, single-family discipline is deliberately restrained. Also avoid if brand-accuracy to Tawuniya specifically matters and a real logo/font asset is required — this template documents its font substitution and missing-logo gap openly.

## Preview Ingredients

- Palette: bg #FFFFFF; ink #0E2841; primary (violet) #6B47F5; deep navy-violet #322A5E; lavender #D6D1ED; mint #D5FCE8; coral #FF6365
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans (weights 300-800) for every role — one family, weight-only hierarchy, standing in for the brand's unavailable custom "Tawuniya" face. See full design doc after selection.
- Signature move: One hero accent (violet) carries every eyebrow, numeral, CTA, active state, and progress indicator — no second accent color.
- Signature move: Single type family end-to-end, differentiated only by weight — mirrors the real brand's own font system.
- Signature move: Mint is rationed to one confidence-callout card per slide, never a background wash.
- Signature move: Deep navy-violet full-bleed panels mark section-dividers and the closing slide — a deliberate surface-color shift, not a card.
- Signature move: Eight layout patterns carried directly from the source brand deck — agenda, section-divider, quote (plain + with photo), timeline, milestones, mind-map, profile card, thank-you close.
